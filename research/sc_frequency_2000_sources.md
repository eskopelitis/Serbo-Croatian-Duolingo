# Serbo-Croatian vocabulary candidates: source and method

This is a **machine-joined, unreviewed candidate set**, not a verified curriculum or a balanced frequency list for all four standards. It is intended for editorial review before learners are graded against it. Automated extraction can contain wrong or missing meanings, odd sense order, corpus tagging mistakes, forms attributed to the wrong sense, proper names, and unsuitable content. A dictionary definition is evidence, not proof of a dependable answer key. No native-speaker review or comprehensive item-by-item review has been done.

Subtitle samples are not independent balanced corpora; they contain spelling variation, inflections, names, possible profanity and subtitles of translated media. Headword surface counts are supplementary only and **do not determine rank**. Zero means absence from the downloaded top-50k list, not absence from the language. There is no Montenegrin corpus in these inputs.

## Reproduction and ranking

Download the files above into a directory as `hrLex_v1.3.gz`, `dictionary.jsonl`, `sr.txt`, `hr.txt`, and `bs.txt`. Verify the recorded SHA-256 hashes and the availability/terms of these versions. From the project root run:

```powershell
python -X utf8 tools/build_sc_frequency_2000.py <input-directory> <output-json>
```

The generator normalizes pitch accents while preserving lexical diacritics such as č, ć, š, ž, đ, ś and ź, transliterates Cyrillic for matching, and joins Wiktionary headwords and senses to hrLex lemma and compatible part of speech. Each score is the sum of matching tagged hrWaC wordform–lemma–POS counts. It sorts descending by that count, breaking ties alphabetically, and takes the first 2,000 *dictionary-matched* candidates. This is a filtered Croatian web-corpus ranking, not a CEFR list, not a ranking of every word in the corpus, and not a claim that one common order applies to all four standards. A small explicit list of spelling counterparts is combined by the generator; no country-specific usage is inferred automatically. Many other variants are not yet connected. Wiktionary identifies `oni`, `mi` and `vi` as plural forms of `on`, `ja` and `ti`; the generator attaches them to those headwords as grammatical forms and preserves their senses, instead of treating them as spellings. Other paradigm overlaps need review. Definitions are preserved by sense and POS, along with extraction labels and source URLs. Categories are ordered by their corpus POS frequency, but senses within each category are not ordered by frequency or learner suitability. Corpus-observed inflections are selected from the 30 most frequent forms per item and require a matching dictionary paradigm or explicit attached lemma; an attached form is not necessarily an acceptable answer for every sense. This conservative filter is incomplete. Ranks, counts, forms and glosses are all subject to source and joining errors.

Four entries include independently sourced, sense-specific regional alternatives: `kruh` / `hleb` / `hljeb` (bread), `vlak` / `voz` (train), `tisuća` / `hiljada` (thousand), and `tjedan` / `sedmica` (week). The alternative may not itself occur among the 2,000 candidates; its Wiktionary URL and source usage tags are included on the entry. These are alternatives for the indicated sense only, not universal substitutions. The dictionary's regional labels are evidence about usage, not claims that all speakers in a country use one form.

## Reading and listening examples

The existing [`tatoeba-pilot.json`](tatoeba-pilot.json) contains 13 selected source/English sentence pairs with individual contributor, sentence URL, and license metadata. The [Tatoeba downloads page](https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads) identifies text exports as CC BY 2.0 FR with some CC0 material; preserve attribution for both sides. Treat the selected pairs as editorially selected, not native-speaker verified. Tatoeba audio licenses vary by contributor; check the individual audio record before using it. [Belgrade's Serbian language center](https://learnserbian.fil.bg.ac.rs/) provides exam and audio materials that can be linked for study; no license to copy its exam items or audio has been established.

## Required review before learner use

Check the English senses against the linked source in context, especially high-frequency function words, polysemy and homonyms. Check that each regional spelling is attached to the right sense, that inflections and diacritics are correct, and that an answer is not accepted solely because it appears in a corpus paradigm. Add clearly sourced Serbian, Bosnian and Montenegrin vocabulary and regional notes rather than treating Croatian source frequency as universal. Review rights, attribution, offensive items and accessibility before bundling. Record native-speaker review and any corrections; do not silently promote `unreviewed_machine_join` entries to a validated answer key.
